ABOUT GMT

A construction company that stays close to the project.

GMT Associates is a commercial general contractor and construction management firm serving clients across the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic.

Based in Trevose, Pennsylvania, GMT combines project management, specialized trade partners, and crews that perform demolition, carpentry, concrete, and structural work directly.

Our experience spans commercial buildings, industrial environments, offices, warehouses, retail, hospitality, specialty recreation, and adaptive reuse.

THE GMT APPROACH

Direct access to the people leading your project.

  • Self-perform capability on critical scopes

  • Field documentation, quality control, and closeout
