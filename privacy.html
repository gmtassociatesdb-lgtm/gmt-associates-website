Website privacy.

How this website handles visits and inquiry drafts.

Updated September 8, 2026. This notice covers the GMT Associates, Inc. website hosted on GitHub Pages.

Project inquiry form

The form prepares an email using the details you enter, such as your name, company, contact information, project location, and project description. The page does not submit those details to a GMT web server. You must review and send the draft in your email application for GMT to receive it. Your browser or email application may retain draft information under its own settings.

If you choose Copy Inquiry, the draft is placed on your device’s clipboard. Include only information needed to begin a project discussion; arrange separately with GMT before sending sensitive documents.

Email communication

When you send an email, GMT receives the message and sender information through its email provider. Your email provider and GMT’s provider process that communication. Contact GMT with questions about handling an inquiry or to request a correction or deletion of information you sent. Applicable recordkeeping requirements may affect what can be deleted.

Website hosting

GitHub hosts this website and may process technical information such as IP addresses and request details to operate and secure its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Cookies and tracking

This website’s own code does not set cookies or use advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Hosting providers, browsers, email applications, and websites you follow through links have their own practices.
